A familiar street can look different when we slow down. A plant has grown through a crack in the sidewalk. Someone has painted a door blue. The afternoon light reaches a window that was dark in the morning.

Paying attention gives ordinary things a little more room. Sometimes that is enough for a short walk to become a story.
